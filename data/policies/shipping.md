# Shipping Policy — v1.0

## Delivery Timelines
- Standard delivery takes 3–7 business days. Express delivery takes 1–3 business days where available.
- Delays during peak season are announced on the tracking page.

## Tracking
- Every order gets a tracking link once the carrier picks up the parcel.
- If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support for an investigation.

## Damaged or Lost Parcels
- Report damage within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item and packaging.
- Lost parcels are investigated with the carrier before a replacement or refund is approved.
