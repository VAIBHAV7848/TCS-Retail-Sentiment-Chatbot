# Refunds and Escalation Policy — v1.0

## Refunds
- Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
- Support agents must verify the case before promising any refund or compensation.
- Never promise instant refunds, charge reversals, or cash compensation in chat.

## Escalation
- Urgent issues (safety, legal threats, high-value orders) are handed off to a human specialist.
- When escalating, share a short summary, key signals, and relevant policy references.
- The customer must be told clearly that a representative will follow up.
