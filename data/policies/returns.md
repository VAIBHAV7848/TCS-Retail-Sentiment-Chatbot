# Returns Policy — v1.0

## Eligibility
- Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Final-sale items and opened personal-care products are not eligible for return.

## Process
- Start a return from your orders page and choose a reason. A prepaid label is issued where available.
- Pack the item securely and drop it off with the assigned carrier.

## Inspection
- Returns are inspected within 5 business days of arrival at the warehouse.
- If the item fails inspection, support will contact you with next steps.
